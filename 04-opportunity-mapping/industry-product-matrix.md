# Industry–Product Fit Matrix

## Purpose

This matrix provides an initial hypothesis of how ISDS products may fit different industries.

The ratings are preliminary and must be validated through market research, customer interviews, technical assessment, and pilot projects.

## Rating Legend

- **A** — Strong initial fit hypothesis
- **B** — Potential fit requiring further validation
- **C** — Possible fit with a more specific use case
- **TBD** — Not yet assessed

## Validation Requirements

Before using this matrix for investment or sales decisions, assess each industry–product combination based on:

1. Evidence of a real customer problem
2. Customer willingness to pay
3. Product capability and technical feasibility
4. Data availability and integration complexity
5. Security and regulatory constraints
6. Availability of a pilot customer
7. Repeatability across multiple customers

Ratings are working assumptions, not evidence of demand, product readiness, or commercial success. They should be revised as evidence is collected.
