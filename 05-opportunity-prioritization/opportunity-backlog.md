## Opportunity Statuses

- **Idea:** Initial opportunity identified.
- **Researching:** Market and customer problem under investigation.
- **Needs Validation:** Initial hypothesis requires customer evidence.
- **Validated:** Evidence supports the existence and importance of the problem.
- **Pilot Proposed:** A pilot concept and scope have been prepared.
- **Pilot Approved:** Relevant stakeholders have approved the pilot.
- **In Pilot:** Pilot execution is underway.
- **Commercially Validated:** Pilot results support a commercial business case.
- **On Hold:** Temporarily paused.
- **Rejected:** Not proceeding, with rationale documented.

## Required Information for Each Opportunity

1. Opportunity ID
2. Industry and subindustry
3. Target customer segment
4. Customer problem and current workaround
5. Evidence that the problem exists
6. Proposed AI solution
7. Candidate ISDS product or product combination
8. Expected business outcomes
9. Potential buyer and decision-maker
10. Data, integration, security, and regulatory requirements
11. Pilot scope, timeline, and success criteria
12. Weighted score and confidence level
13. Owner and next action
14. Current status and decision rationale

## Prioritization

Use the scoring method defined in `scoring-model.md`.
