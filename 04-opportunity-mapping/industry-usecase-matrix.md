# Industry–Use Case Opportunity Matrix

## Purpose

This matrix identifies potential AI use cases across the target industries.

It is intended to guide market research and customer discovery, not to confirm demand.

## Legend

- **H** — High initial relevance hypothesis
- **M** — Medium initial relevance hypothesis
- **L** — Lower initial relevance hypothesis
- **TBD** — Not assessed

These ratings represent preliminary assumptions and require validation.

## How to Use This Matrix

1. Select an industry.
2. Identify its most relevant use-case families.
3. Research actual customer problems.
4. Interview potential customers and domain experts.
5. Validate technical feasibility and data readiness.
6. Create a separate opportunity record for each promising problem.

A high rating does not prove market demand, willingness to pay, or product readiness. Update ratings as reliable evidence becomes available.
