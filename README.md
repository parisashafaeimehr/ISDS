# ISDS AI Product Strategy

**A strategic framework for AI product portfolio management, market development, and project governance.**

---

## Overview

This repository provides a structured framework for identifying market opportunities, evaluating AI product capabilities, designing industry-specific solutions, and managing AI initiatives from discovery to commercialization.

The framework connects product strategy, industry analysis, use-case discovery, opportunity prioritization, project execution, and governance in a unified structure.

## Objectives

- Map the existing ISDS AI product portfolio and reusable capabilities.
- Identify and assess opportunities across target industries.
- Connect industry needs with product capabilities and AI use cases.
- Prioritize opportunities based on business value, feasibility, and market access.
- Standardize project planning, responsibilities, risk management, and performance measurement.
- Support the development of scalable, commercially viable AI solutions.

## Framework

| Workstream | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Product Portfolio | Document products, capabilities, and potential overlaps |
| Industry Analysis | Define target industries, sub-industries, and market segments |
| Use-Case Mapping | Connect industry problems with potential AI applications |
| Opportunity Assessment | Evaluate and prioritize business opportunities |
| Project Management | Structure project phases, tasks, and deliverables |
| RACI & Governance | Clarify responsibilities and decision ownership |
| Risk Management | Identify, assess, mitigate, and monitor project risks |
| KPI & Performance | Define success criteria and measure realized value |

## Repository Structure

- `docs/` — Strategic framework and operating guidelines
- `matrices/` — Industry, product, use-case, and responsibility matrices
- `templates/` — Reusable Excel templates for analysis and project execution
- `portfolio/` — Product catalog and capability documentation
- `use-cases/` — AI use-case taxonomy and solution opportunities
- `projects/` — Project-specific plans, deliverables, and pilot documentation

## Opportunity-to-Value Lifecycle

1. **Discover** — Identify industries, customers, and business problems.
2. **Assess** — Validate market demand, strategic fit, data readiness, and feasibility.
3. **Prioritize** — Rank opportunities using explicit evaluation criteria.
4. **Design** — Define the solution, MVP scope, architecture, and success metrics.
5. **Develop** — Build, integrate, and validate the solution.
6. **Pilot** — Test the solution in a representative business environment.
7. **Commercialize** — Evaluate business outcomes, deployment readiness, and scalability.

## Working Principles

- Reuse existing capabilities before creating new products.
- Validate customer needs before committing to full-scale development.
- Separate hypotheses from validated market evidence.
- Define measurable acceptance criteria before each pilot.
- Assign clear ownership for every critical activity and decision.
- Incorporate security, data governance, and legal requirements from the beginning.
- Evaluate success through measurable customer and business value.

## Governance Note

Industry-product mappings, opportunity scores, and initial prioritizations are working hypotheses until validated through customer discovery, technical assessment, and market evidence.

Actual product capabilities, implementation maturity, and commercial readiness must be assessed separately before making delivery commitments.
