# Industry Taxonomy

## Purpose

This document defines the initial industry classification for identifying market opportunities for ISDS AI products.

The taxonomy is a starting point and may be refined based on market research, customer access, and business priorities.

## Segmentation Principles

Industries should be evaluated using:

- Business needs and operational challenges
- AI adoption maturity
- Data availability and quality
- Security and regulatory requirements
- Market size and growth potential
- Accessibility of potential customers
- Fit with the ISDS product portfolio

## Important Note

This taxonomy is a preliminary classification. It does not represent validated market demand or a final market prioritization.
