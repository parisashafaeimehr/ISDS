# Project Charter

## 1. Project Overview

This project establishes a structured framework for AI product strategy, market development, opportunity prioritization, and commercialization at ISDS.

## 2. Objectives

- Understand the existing ISDS AI product portfolio.
- Identify potential industries and customer needs.
- Map AI products to industry opportunities.
- Prioritize opportunities based on business value and feasibility.
- Define project roles, responsibilities, and governance.
- Support the design and execution of AI pilots.

## 3. Scope

The project covers:

- Product portfolio analysis
- Industry and market analysis
- Use-case identification
- Opportunity assessment and prioritization
- Project planning and governance
- Risk and KPI management
- Pilot planning and commercialization

## 4. Key Deliverables

- Product catalog
- Industry taxonomy
- Industry-product matrix
- Use-case catalog
- Opportunity backlog
- Project WBS and RACI matrix
- Risk register and KPI framework
- Pilot project plans

## 5. Project Status

Initial framework — under development.
