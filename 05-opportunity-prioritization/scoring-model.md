# Opportunity Scoring Model

## 1. Purpose

This model provides a consistent method for comparing AI business opportunities and deciding which ones deserve further investigation or pilot development.

## 2. Scoring Scale

Each criterion is scored from 1 to 5.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Very low |
| 2 | Low |
| 3 | Medium |
| 4 | High |
| 5 | Very high |

## 3. Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Guiding Question |
|---|---:|---|
| Economic Value | 25% | How significant is the potential business value for the customer? |
| Customer Access | 15% | Can we reach decision-makers and potential pilot customers? |
| Technical Readiness | 15% | Can the solution be built with available technology and capabilities? |
| Pilot Speed | 15% | How quickly can a meaningful pilot be delivered? |
| Repeatability | 15% | Can the solution be reused across multiple customers? |
| ISDS Portfolio Fit | 15% | How well does the opportunity fit the current ISDS products and strategy? |
| **Total** | **100%** | |

## 4. Calculation

For each opportunity:

**Weighted Score = Sum of (Criterion Score × Criterion Weight)**

Because the weights total 100%, the final score ranges from 1 to 5.

Optional conversion to a 100-point scale:

**Opportunity Score = Weighted Score × 20**

## 5. Initial Decision Thresholds

| Score out of 100 | Suggested Action |
|---|---|
| 80–100 | Prioritize for validation and pilot planning |
| 65–79 | Investigate further |
| 50–64 | Keep in the backlog |
| Below 50 | Deprioritize unless strategic reasons justify further work |

These thresholds are initial guidelines and should be calibrated using actual project outcomes.

## 6. Mandatory Risk Checks

A high score does not automatically mean an opportunity should proceed.

Before approval, assess:

- Data access and data quality
- Information security and privacy
- Legal and regulatory requirements
- Integration complexity
- Model reliability and evaluation requirements
- Customer commitment and pilot acceptance criteria

Critical security, legal, or feasibility issues must be resolved before proceeding, regardless of the score.

## 7. Evidence and Confidence

Every score should include:

- Evidence or source
- Assumptions
- Confidence level: Low, Medium, or High
- Name of the evaluator
- Assessment date

Do not assign high confidence to scores based only on assumptions.

## 8. Governance

The responsible opportunity owner prepares the assessment. Relevant product, technical, commercial, security, and domain stakeholders review it before a pilot decision.

## Status

Initial scoring framework — subject to review and calibration.
