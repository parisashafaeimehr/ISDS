# AI Product Development — Work Breakdown Structure

## Purpose

This WBS defines the major activities required to investigate, develop, validate, and commercialize an AI solution.

The exact activities depend on the product, customer, project size, and deployment environment.

## Phase 1 — Discovery & Strategy

- 1.1 Identify the target industry and customer segment
- 1.2 Research the market and competitors
- 1.3 Identify customer problems
- 1.4 Conduct customer and domain-expert interviews
- 1.5 Define the value proposition
- 1.6 Assess strategic fit with ISDS
- 1.7 Prepare the initial opportunity assessment

## Phase 2 — Requirements & Feasibility

- 2.1 Define business requirements
- 2.2 Document user journeys and workflows
- 2.3 Define functional requirements
- 2.4 Assess data availability and quality
- 2.5 Assess technical feasibility
- 2.6 Assess security, privacy, and regulatory requirements
- 2.7 Define measurable acceptance criteria
- 2.8 Approve the proposed scope

## Phase 3 — Solution Design

- 3.1 Define the solution architecture
- 3.2 Select candidate ISDS products and AI models
- 3.3 Design data flows and integrations
- 3.4 Design user experience
- 3.5 Define evaluation and testing methods
- 3.6 Estimate cost, timeline, and required resources

## Phase 4 — Development & Integration

- 4.1 Prepare the development environment
- 4.2 Implement data and system integrations
- 4.3 Configure or develop AI capabilities
- 4.4 Implement application workflows
- 4.5 Implement access controls and security measures
- 4.6 Prepare logging and monitoring
- 4.7 Document technical decisions

## Phase 5 — Testing & Evaluation

- 5.1 Prepare test data and test cases
- 5.2 Test functional requirements
- 5.3 Evaluate AI output quality
- 5.4 Test reliability and failure handling
- 5.5 Test security and permissions
- 5.6 Conduct user acceptance testing
- 5.7 Fix critical issues and retest

## Phase 6 — Pilot Execution

- 6.1 Confirm pilot customer and participants
- 6.2 Agree on baseline measurements
- 6.3 Define pilot duration and scope
- 6.4 Deploy the pilot solution
- 6.5 Train users
- 6.6 Monitor performance and collect feedback
- 6.7 Measure results against acceptance criteria

## Phase 7 — Commercialization

- 7.1 Evaluate pilot results and business value
- 7.2 Define pricing and commercial model
- 7.3 Prepare sales and demonstration materials
- 7.4 Define support and service commitments
- 7.5 Assess repeatability across customers
- 7.6 Prepare the go-to-market plan
- 7.7 Make the scale, revise, or stop decision

## Phase 8 — Operations & Continuous Improvement

- 8.1 Monitor availability and performance
- 8.2 Monitor AI quality and business outcomes
- 8.3 Manage incidents and user feedback
- 8.4 Maintain models, prompts, and integrations
- 8.5 Review security and access controls
- 8.6 Track costs and resource consumption
- 8.7 Plan improvements and releases

## Governance Notes

- Each task must have an accountable owner.
- Deliverables and acceptance criteria must be defined before execution.
- Tasks may be added, removed, or reordered based on project needs.
- Security, privacy, and compliance activities must not be postponed until the end.

## Status

Initial WBS — to be tailored for each approved opportunity or pilot.
