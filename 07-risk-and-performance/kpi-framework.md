# AI Product KPI Framework

## Purpose

This framework defines how ISDS AI opportunities, pilots, products, and commercialization efforts should be evaluated.

KPIs must be selected according to the project objectives and agreed upon before execution.

## 1. Market & Customer KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified Opportunities | Opportunities meeting initial qualification criteria | Count |
| Customer Interviews | Completed interviews with target customers or domain experts | Count |
| Problem Validation Rate | Percentage of researched opportunities with sufficient evidence of a real problem | Validated opportunities / researched opportunities |
| Pilot Conversion Rate | Percentage of qualified opportunities that progress to an approved pilot | Approved pilots / qualified opportunities |
| Willingness-to-Pay Evidence | Evidence that target customers may pay for the proposed solution | Documented findings |

## 2. Product & Delivery KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Time to First Pilot | Time from approved scope to pilot launch | Days |
| Delivery Predictability | Degree to which milestones are delivered as planned | On-time milestones / total milestones |
| Requirement Coverage | Percentage of agreed requirements that pass acceptance testing | Accepted requirements / total requirements |
| Pilot Completion Rate | Percentage of started pilots completed against the agreed scope | Completed pilots / started pilots |
| Critical Defects | Number of unresolved critical defects | Count |

## 3. AI Quality KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Task Success Rate | Percentage of evaluated tasks completed successfully | Successful tasks / total evaluated tasks |
| Answer Quality | Quality of AI outputs against an agreed evaluation rubric | Evaluation score |
| Grounded Answer Rate | Percentage of answers adequately supported by approved sources, where relevant | Grounded answers / evaluated answers |
| Hallucination Rate | Frequency of unsupported or fabricated outputs in evaluated cases | Failed cases / evaluated cases |
| Human Escalation Rate | Percentage of cases requiring human intervention | Escalated cases / total cases |

AI quality metrics must be selected according to the solution type. Not every KPI applies to every product.

## 4. Business Value KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Time Saved | Reduction in time required to complete a target task | Baseline time minus measured time |
| Cost Reduction | Reduction in the cost of performing a target process | Baseline cost minus measured cost |
| Productivity Improvement | Change in completed work per unit of time | Compare baseline and pilot |
| User Adoption | Percentage of intended users actively using the solution | Active users / intended users |
| User Satisfaction | User-reported satisfaction with the solution | Survey score |
| Business Outcome Achievement | Degree to which agreed business targets are achieved | Actual result versus target |

## 5. Commercialization KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot-to-Customer Conversion | Percentage of completed pilots that become paying customers | Paying customers / completed pilots |
| Sales Cycle Duration | Time from qualified opportunity to commercial decision | Days |
| Revenue | Revenue attributable to the product or solution | Financial records |
| Gross Margin | Revenue less applicable cost of delivery, divided by revenue | Percentage |
| Customer Retention | Percentage of customers retained over a defined period | Retained customers / starting customers |
| Reusability Rate | Extent to which a solution can be deployed for another customer without major redevelopment | Defined assessment rubric |

## KPI Governance

For every selected KPI, document:

- KPI name and business purpose
- Formula and measurement unit
- Baseline value
- Target value
- Data source
- Measurement frequency
- Responsible owner
- Reporting period
- Acceptance threshold

Targets should be agreed with relevant stakeholders before a pilot begins. Do not invent targets without a baseline or business justification.

## Evaluation Principle

A successful AI pilot should demonstrate measurable business value, acceptable AI quality, operational feasibility, security compliance, and a credible path toward commercialization.

A technically successful demonstration does not automatically establish commercial viability.
