# Project Role Catalog

## Purpose

This catalog defines the roles that may contribute to AI product strategy, development, delivery, and commercialization.

One person may perform multiple roles, depending on project size and organizational structure.

## Roles

| ID | Role | Main Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| SP | Sponsor | Provides strategic direction and approves major decisions |
| PM | Project Manager | Plans and coordinates project delivery |
| PO | Product Owner | Defines product priorities and manages the product backlog |
| BA | Business Analyst | Analyzes business processes and requirements |
| SA | Solution Architect | Designs the overall solution architecture |
| AI | AI/ML Engineer | Develops and evaluates AI and machine-learning capabilities |
| DE | Data Engineer | Builds data pipelines and data integrations |
| SWE | Software Engineer | Develops application and backend components |
| UX | UX/UI Designer | Designs user experience and interfaces |
| QA | Quality Assurance | Plans and performs quality testing |
| SEC | Security Specialist | Assesses security, privacy, and access controls |
| OPS | Operations / MLOps Engineer | Supports deployment, monitoring, and operations |
| DA | Data Analyst | Analyzes data and measures business outcomes |
| SME | Subject Matter Expert | Provides industry and domain knowledge |
| LEG | Legal / Compliance | Reviews legal, contractual, and regulatory requirements |
| FIN | Finance Specialist | Evaluates costs, financial benefits, and business cases |
| SALE | Sales / Business Development | Identifies customers and develops commercial opportunities |
| CS | Customer Success | Supports onboarding, adoption, and customer outcomes |
| CUST | Customer Representative | Represents customer needs and provides feedback |
| ITC | Customer IT / Integration | Supports customer systems, infrastructure, and integrations |
| UAT | User Acceptance Tester | Validates the solution against user needs and acceptance criteria |

## Role Assignment Principles

- Every project must have a clearly identified accountable decision-maker.
- Every major deliverable must have an owner.
- Security, legal, and compliance specialists must be involved when relevant.
- Customer representatives should participate in requirements validation and pilot evaluation.
- Roles should be assigned according to actual capabilities and availability.
