# AI Use Case Catalog

## Purpose

This catalog identifies reusable AI use-case families that can be applied across different industries.

The objective is to connect real business problems with potential ISDS products and solutions.

## Use Case Families

| ID | Use Case Family | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| U01 | Knowledge & Document Intelligence | Extract, search, summarize, and answer questions from organizational documents | Search internal policies and contracts |
| U02 | AI Agents | Execute multi-step tasks using tools, data, and business systems | An agent that prepares a procurement report |
| U03 | Business Intelligence & Analytics | Analyze business data and explain performance | Ask questions about sales in natural language |
| U04 | Process Automation | Automate repetitive workflows and approvals | Automate invoice processing |
| U05 | Market & Strategy Intelligence | Monitor competitors, markets, and strategic indicators | Track competitor activity |
| U06 | Executive Assistance | Help executives access information and prepare decisions | Generate a management briefing |
| U07 | Learning & Education | Personalize learning and support educational activities | Student learning assistant |
| U08 | AI Development & Infrastructure | Build, test, deploy, and manage AI applications | Develop an internal AI application |
| U09 | Computer Vision & OCR | Interpret images, scanned documents, and visual information | Extract data from scanned forms |
| U10 | Voice & Conversational AI | Process speech and support spoken interactions | Voice-based customer support |
| U11 | Forecasting & Optimization | Predict future outcomes and optimize decisions | Demand forecasting and inventory planning |
| U12 | Customer Interaction | Support customer service, sales, and customer experience | AI assistant for customer inquiries |
| U13 | Risk, Security & Compliance | Identify risks and support compliance activities | Review documents against internal policies |
| U14 | Research & Scientific Discovery | Support scientific research, experimentation, and discovery | Literature analysis for drug discovery |

## Use Case Assessment Dimensions

Each use case should eventually be evaluated using:

- Business value
- Frequency and severity of the problem
- Customer willingness to pay
- Data readiness
- Technical feasibility
- Integration complexity
- Security and regulatory requirements
- Pilot implementation time
- Reusability across customers
- Fit with the ISDS portfolio

## Important Note

These are use-case categories, not confirmed product features or validated market opportunities. Each opportunity requires further investigation.
