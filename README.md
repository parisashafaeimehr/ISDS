# ISDS AI Product Strategy

A structured framework for AI product strategy, market development, opportunity assessment, and project governance.

## Objectives

- Map the existing ISDS AI product portfolio.
- Identify opportunities across target industries.
- Connect industry needs with AI products and use cases.
- Prioritize business opportunities.
- Define project responsibilities using RACI.
- Manage project risks, KPIs, and deliverables.
- Support the development and commercialization of AI solutions.

## Framework Structure

1. Foundation & Strategy
2. Product Portfolio
3. Market Intelligence
4. Opportunity Mapping
5. Opportunity Prioritization
6. Project Governance
7. Risk Management & Performance
8. AI Projects & Pilots
